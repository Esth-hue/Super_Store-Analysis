# Sample Superstore Business Analysis

## Project Overview
This project presents a business analysis of the Sample Superstore dataset using Microsoft Power BI.
The analysis focuses on sales performance, profitability, regional performance, customer segments, product categories, product profitability, and sales trends over time.
The goal is to transform transactional data into meaningful business insights that can support better commercial and strategic decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Evaluate the company's overall sales and profitability performance.
- Identify the regions generating the highest sales and profit.
- Determine which customer segments contribute the most revenue.
- Evaluate product category performance.
- Identify the most profitable products.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Identify business risks and opportunities.
- Provide actionable recommendations based on the findings.

## Business Questions
- What is the overall sales performance of the company?
- Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which products are the most profitable?
- What trends can be observed over time?

## Data Preparation
The dataset was imported into Power Query in Power BI for data-quality assessment.
The following checks were performed:
- Imported the dataset as a CSV file into Power Query.
- Reviewed the dataset structure.
- Checked for missing values.
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Checked data types.
  
The dataset was found to be intact and suitable for analysis, so no major data-cleaning or imputation was required before loading the data into Power Bi.

## Key Insights
- Sales recovered strongly after the 2015 decline
  
Sales decreased slightly from $484,247.50 in 2014 to $470,532.51 in 2015, then rose to $609,205.60 in 2016 and $733,215.26 in 2017. Overall, sales increased by approximately 51% from 2014 to 2017, indicating strong recovery and renewed growth momentum.

- West is the strongest-performing region
  
West generated approximately $0.73M in sales and $0.11M in profit, the highest among the regions shown. East followed with $0.68M in sales and $0.09M profit, while Central and South recorded lower sales and profit.

-	Consumer customers drive the largest share of revenue
  
Consumers generated approximately $1.16M, representing 50.56% of total sales. Corporate contributed $0.71M (30.74%), while Home Office contributed $0.43M (18.70%). Consumer is therefore the company's largest revenue base.

- Technology leads category performance
  
Technology recorded approximately $0.84M in sales and $0.15M in profit, making it the strongest category on both measures. Furniture generated about $0.74M in sales but only $0.02M in profit, showing that strong sales volume does not necessarily translate into strong profitability.

- Profitability is concentrated among a few top products
  
The Top 5 Products by Profit visual shows a clear gap between the leading product and the other products. The leading product contributes approximately $25K in profit, followed by products contributing about $8K, $7K, $5K and $4K. Product profitability is therefore uneven.

## Business Risks
- Low furniture profitability
  
Furniture has relatively high sales but very low profit. This may indicate pricing, discount, or cost issues.

- Revenue concentration risk
  
 Consumer accounts for 50.56% of total sales. A significant decline in Consumer demand could therefore have a material impact on overall revenue.

- Regional performance gap
  
The difference between West and weaker regions such as South and Central indicates that performance is not evenly distributed, which may constrain future growth.

## Business Opportunities
- Develop weaker regions
  
Target Central and South regions with focused sales campaigns and market-development initiatives.

- Expand other segments
  
Increase engagement with Corporate and Home Office customers while retaining high-value customers.

- Improve Furniture margin
  
Furniture's strong sales but low profit create an opportunity to improve margins through better optimization of pricing, discount, product selection and cost control.

## Actionable Recommendation
- Investigate the 2015 sales decline
  
Compare products, regions and customer segments that underperformed in 2015 with those that drove the stronger results in 2016 and 2017.

- Prioritize high-profit Technology products
  
 Increase marketing and sales attention on Technology products with strong margins while monitoring profitability as sales grow.

- Prioritize high-profit product
  
 Focus marketing, inventory, and sales efforts on the top 5 most profitable products to maximize returns.

- Strengthen regional performance
  
Investigate the causes of the gap and implement targeted promotions and sales strategies to improve their performance.

- Strengthen and diversify the customer base
  
Develop targeted marketing and promotional strategies for corporate and Home Office customers while retaining high-value customers.

## Conclusion
The Sample Superstore analysis indicates a business with strong overall sales, positive profitability and significant growth momentum after the 2015 decline.
The strongest performance is concentrated in the West region, Consumer segment and Technology category. At the same time, Furniture profitability and uneven regional performance require management attention.
